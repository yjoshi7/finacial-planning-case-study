Recommendations

  1. Increase emergency fund to at least 6 months of expenses (₹4.5 Lakhs).
  2. Enhance term insurance to ₹2.5 Crore to cover the HLV gap.
  3. Start goal‑based SIPs totalling ₹42,000/month for education and retirement.
  4. Follow the illustrative asset allocation – 70% Equity, 25% Debt, 5% Gold.
  5. Review the plan annually or after major life events.
Recommendations are prioritised: immediate (emergency, insurance) → short‑term (start SIPs) → ongoing (review). This mirrors how an advisor would present an action plan.
Assumptions & Methodology
  • Inflation: 6% (general), 9% (education)
  • Expected return: 10%
  • Retirement age: 60, Life expectancy: 85
  • HLV = Annual income × (1 – (1+return)^-years) / return (simplified)
  • SIP formula: [Shortfall × r/12] / [(1+r/12)^(n) – 1]
